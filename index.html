About me

My route into marketing ran through sales. Three years selling event technology to enterprise clients at Micepad taught me to start with what the buyer actually needs to know. Since 2020 I've applied that to search, across engineering, consulting, proptech and now commodities recruitment.

Today I'm Senior Marketing Manager at Kepler Search, a specialist recruiter for the commodities and trading sector, where I lead brand, content, SEO and the website. I'm most interested in the shift from ranking pages to being the source AI answers are built from, and I write about it on my blog.

How I work

Four steps I use for every page that matters

  1. Find the real questions

    Start from what customers ask, in search data and in AI tools, not from a keyword list alone.

  2. Build the best answer

    One focused page per question, with a direct answer up top and something original underneath.

  3. Make it machine-readable

    Fast, crawlable pages with clear headings, named authors and schema markup that connects the brand.

  4. Measure both channels

    Track rankings and traffic, and check regularly how AI tools describe the brand and the topic.
