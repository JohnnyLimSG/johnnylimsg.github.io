Why hire in-house instead of an agency or freelancer?

Agencies and freelancers are great for one-off projects. But SEO and GEO tend to work best when someone is inside the business, every day:

  • I'd know your market. The content that gets ranked and cited comes from talking to your sales team, consultants and customers every week. That's hard to do from outside.
  • I'd build things only you can publish. Original surveys, case studies and expert insights are what AI answers quote, and they need someone on the inside to make them happen.
  • I'd fix the website directly. No waiting on tickets or change requests. Technical SEO issues get found and fixed in the same week.
  • One person owns the result. Rankings, AI visibility and the enquiries they bring in sit with one person who's accountable for them.

What would I do in my first 90 days?

  1. Review what's already published and how it fits your brand.
  2. Audit the technical SEO, so new work isn't wasted.
  3. Talk to the people who know your market best, and turn that into a content plan.
  4. Check how Google, ChatGPT and Perplexity describe your company today, and set up tracking in Search Console, Google Analytics and Screaming Frog.

FAQ

What's the difference between SEO, GEO, AEO and AIO?

SEO (search engine optimisation) gets your pages ranked in Google. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) gets your brand used and cited in AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) focuses on structuring content so it's picked as the direct answer to a question. AIO is used both for AI optimisation in general and for Google's AI Overviews specifically. In practice they overlap heavily, and I work across all of them. Full comparison of SEO, GEO, AEO and AIO.
